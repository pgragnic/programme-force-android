Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Programme Force is a free app with no ads, no trackers and no analytics. It is published by Philippe G., who can be reached at pgragnic@hotmail.com.

Without an account: everything stays on your phone

You can use the app without an account. Your sessions, activity days and settings are then stored only on your phone and are not sent to anyone.

With an account: online backup

If you create an account, the following data is backed up online so you can find it on another phone:

  • your email address and password (the password is never stored in plain text);
  • your sessions: date, exercises, loads, repetitions, perceived difficulty, and, if you enter it, your body weight and estimated energy expenditure;
  • your activity days: date, step count, activity type (weighted walk, hike, trail run), backpack weight, duration, elevation gain;
  • your settings: current cycle and week, equipment, rest time;
  • weekly summaries.

This data is hosted by Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), acting as a processor. Each account can only read and change its own data. It is used only to run the app: it is never sold, rented, shared or used for advertising.

Health Connect (step count)

If you allow it, the app reads your daily step count from Health Connect, and nothing else. It uses it to show your activity, estimate your energy expenditure and compute leg fatigue. Steps are stored on your phone and, if you have an account, in your online backup. The app does not write anything to Health Connect.

Use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements: it is only used for the app features described here, never transferred to third parties, and never used for advertising or creditworthiness.

Phone permissions

  • Internet: online backup and exercise programme updates;
  • Notifications and foreground service: timer and controls of the current session, session reminder;
  • Vibration and keeping the processor awake: cues during the session, even with the screen off;
  • Phone startup: rescheduling the session reminder.

The session voice uses your phone's text-to-speech; no audio is recorded.

Retention and deletion

Online data is kept as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all your data at any time from the app (Infos tab → Delete my account) or by following the Delete my account page. Deletion is immediate and permanent. Data kept on the phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data by writing to pgragnic@hotmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Health and age

Programme Force is not a medical device and does not replace medical advice. It is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated and significant changes are announced in the app.
