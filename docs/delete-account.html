Delete my UpRep account

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open UpRep and go to the Infos tab.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm: your account and all your online data are deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email pgragnic@hotmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my account". Deletion is done within 7 days and confirmed by email.

What is deleted

Your account (email address and password), sessions, activity days, settings and weekly summaries: everything is permanently deleted, nothing is kept.

Data kept on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
