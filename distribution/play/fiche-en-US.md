## Full description (4000 max) — 1657 caractères

```
UpRep helps you get back in shape at home, with no gym and no ads.

★ WITH THE EQUIPMENT YOU HAVE
Dumbbells, kettlebell, barbell, resistance bands, bench, pull-up bar or just your bodyweight: tick what you own and UpRep builds your workouts from 77 exercises.

★ ADAPTED TO YOUR JOINTS
Knee, back or shoulder trouble? Exercises that load it are swapped for gentler variations.

★ GUIDED FROM START TO FINISH
A 3D character demonstrates every move and highlights the muscles worked. A voice coaches you: up, hold, down. The timer handles sets and rest, even with the screen off.

★ AN AI COACH THAT LISTENS
Tired, short on time, travelling without equipment, a nagging pain? Talk to the coach: it adapts today's workout and explains every change with a before / after. You can undo anything. It debriefs each session and remembers your last 30 days. (Coach powered by Anthropic's Claude, account required.)

★ SAFE PROGRESSION
Starting loads are estimated, then adjusted from your reps and how it felt. Weekly increases are capped, more so for beginners and over-50s.

★ YOUR BODY AT A GLANCE
Recovery for every muscle, muscle balance with catch-up the following week, load and volume charts, steps imported from Health Connect (optional).

★ SIMPLE AND RESPECTFUL
• Free, no ads
• Works without an account; sign in with Google or email for the AI coach, backup and sync
• Account data hosted in Europe, export and account deletion from the app
• English and French, light and dark themes

For home strength training, toning, getting back into exercise or mobility.

UpRep does not replace medical advice. Stop and seek advice if you feel pain or discomfort.
```
